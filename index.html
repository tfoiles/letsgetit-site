History

Let's Get It formed in the summer of 2008 with five friends from Dayton, Ohio [S1]. The band's MySpace profile was created on 4 June 2008 and, by August 2008, was listing dates across the Midwest and Southwest while still unsigned [S8] [S9]. It signed to Fearless Records "just after the band's one-year anniversary" (exact date not stated) [S1] and released the Digital Spaces EP on 4 August 2009 [S3]. The debut album Masters of the Universe followed on 24 May 2011 [S21]. MusicBrainz records the group as ended in 2012 [S2].

The Fearless bio says the band aimed to build a live show "second best to no one" [S1].

"we started to write music that could inspire other people to chase their dreams." — guitarist, as credited on the Fearless bio [S1]; the name printed there conflicts with other sources — see Members
"When people are so supportive, it's hard to try any less than your best." — Joe Gilford, singer [S1]

Fearless Records Connection

Let's Get It signed to Fearless Records "just after the band's one-year anniversary" (exact signing date not stated) [S1]. The Digital Spaces EP was recorded with producer Joey Sturgis [S1] and released on 4 August 2009; Fearless's news ticker on the band's MySpace announced it streaming on PureVolume on 27 July 2009 [S11]. Masters of the Universe came out on Fearless on 24 May 2011 [S21]. In July 2010 the band announced it would record the album in Hollywood, CA with Ian Kirkpatrick and Mike Green [S15]; final production credits are not yet confirmed.

  • Fall 2010 Salt Lake City dates. Concert Archives (S18) lists two consecutive Salt Lake City dates (Oct 22 'The Basement', Oct 23 'Avalon Theater'). Unresolved. [S18]
