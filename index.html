History

Let's Get It was formed in the summer of 2008 by five friends from Dayton, Ohio [S1]. The group signed to Fearless Records just after the band's one-year anniversary; the exact signing date is not stated in the sources [S1].

The band aimed to deliver a live show they would make “second best to no one” [S1].

"we started to write music that could inspire other people to chase their dreams." — Tyler Foiles, guitarist [S1]
"When people are so supportive, it's hard to try any less than your best." — Joe Gilford, singer [S1]
