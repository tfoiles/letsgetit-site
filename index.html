History

Let's Get It formed in the summer of 2008 with five friends from Dayton, Ohio [S1]. The band's MySpace profile was created on 4 June 2008 and, by August 2008, was listing dates across the Midwest and Southwest while still unsigned [S8] [S9]. It signed to Fearless Records "just after the band's one-year anniversary" (exact date not stated) [S1] and released the Digital Spaces EP on 4 August 2009 [S3]. The debut album Masters of the Universe followed on 24 May 2011 [S21]. MusicBrainz records the group as ended in 2012 [S2].

The Fearless bio says the band aimed to build a live show "second best to no one" [S1].

"we started to write music that could inspire other people to chase their dreams." — Tyler Smyth, guitarist [S1] [S22]. The Fearless bio prints the name as "Tyler Foiles", a typo per the band; the attribution to Tyler Smyth follows from the bio calling the speaker "guitarist" and is pending confirmation.
"When people are so supportive, it's hard to try any less than your best." — Joe Gilford, singer [S1]

Fearless Records Connection

Let's Get It signed to Fearless Records "just after the band's one-year anniversary" (exact signing date not stated) [S1]. The Digital Spaces EP was recorded with producer Joey Sturgis [S1] and released on 4 August 2009; Fearless's news ticker on the band's MySpace announced it streaming on PureVolume on 27 July 2009 [S11]. Masters of the Universe came out on Fearless on 24 May 2011 [S21]. In July 2010 the band announced it would record the album in Hollywood, CA with Ian Kirkpatrick and Mike Green [S15]; final production credits are not yet confirmed.

Conflicting information

  • Guitarist's name in the Fearless bio (resolved). The Fearless bio (S1) prints 'guitarist Tyler Foiles'. A band member confirms that is a typo: the lineup is Tyler Smyth (guitar) and Taylor Foiles (bass) (S22), matching S15 and S17. The bio quote is attributed here to Tyler Smyth because the bio calls its speaker 'guitarist'; that attribution is inferred and awaits confirmation. [S1] [S15] [S17] [S22]
  • Masters of the Universe track count. Fearless (S21) lists 13 tracks; MusicBrainz (S3) lists 14, adding 'Fade Out (alternate version)'. This archive shows the 13 official tracks and notes the extra. [S21] [S3]
  • Aug 18, 2010 date location. S15 prints 'St. Paul, MI'; St. Paul is in Minnesota, so 'MI' looks like a typo. Shown as Minnesota with the source spelling noted. [S15]
  • Fall 2010 Salt Lake City dates. Concert Archives (S18) lists two consecutive Salt Lake City dates (Oct 22 'The Basement', Oct 23 'Avalon Theater'). Unresolved. [S18]
  • 2015-01-01 listing. Concert Archives (S18) lists a multi-band event dated 2015-01-01 that includes Let's Get It, although the band is recorded as ended in 2012. Treated as unverified. [S18] [S2]
  • Label status in 2008-09. Archived MySpace (S9, S10) lists the label as 'Unsigned' in August 2008 (S9) and January 2009 (S10); S1 says the band signed about a year after forming. Consistent, but the exact signing date is unknown. [S1] [S9] [S10]
  • Steel City Music Festival date. Concert Archives (S18) lists the festival on both 2009-07-29 and 2009-08-29 at the same Pittsburgh venue; the band's own schedule (S11) says 2009-08-29. The July date looks like a mis-dated duplicate. [S11] [S18]

The 2010 press release (S15) and Concert Archives (S18) disagree on several dates: Aug 6-7 appear in S18 as Transmission Festival / Scream It Like You Mean It bills; Aug 10 (S15: Trocadero Upstairs, Philadelphia; S18: The Balcony); Aug 14 (S15: Evansville; S18: Indianapolis); Aug 15 (S15: La Crosse; S18: Madison); Aug 17 (S18: La Crosse).
